| 1 | **Capture-level sequential e-process for malware presence warnings** (IoT-23) | Active primary; no valid positive POC yet | Reformulate the target to use capture/scenario-level malware labels, independent of the analyst's per-flow scan/C&C behavior labels. The direct fan-out score's AUROC 0.9995 is invalid because it matches the scan-label rule; C&C recurrence/periodicity also failed (AUROC about 0.49). Exact prior art [VANEB](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09074) already provides variance-aware empirical-Bayes personalization for noisy federated local estimates; [FedMSE](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.14121v2) and [G-PFL-ID](https://doi.org/10.3390/iot7010013) further occupy benign-only IoT federation. Retain the dependence/calibration failure as design information; do not promote this estimator formulation without a substantially different mechanism and untouched validation. |

## Acceptance rule

“Active lead” means the next candidate to falsify, not a positive finding. A
candidate becomes accepted only after a specific prior-art check, valid data and
split audit, implemented real-data POC with positive mechanism signal, a simple
baseline challenge, and leakage review. Rejected candidates remain listed so the
search does not silently recycle them.

There are currently **zero accepted candidates**. Rank all active candidates first
by current investigation priority, followed by rejected candidates in the order
they were investigated. Keep every rejected candidate visible; update this table
whenever a candidate is accepted, rejected, or replaced, retaining its decision
evidence in the ledger.
