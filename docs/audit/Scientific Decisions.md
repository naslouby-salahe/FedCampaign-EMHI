# Scientific Decisions

## Reconstructed study

FedCampaign-EMHI asks whether an interaction-based global detector can identify constructed co-temporal distributed malicious episodes before any fixed client-local detector policy acts. For each coalition, it constructs nuisance context from predictable history of clients outside that coalition, estimates the coalition's higher-order innovation after removing proper-subset structure, and aggregates bounded evidence into a global stopping rule. The operational outcome is strict ODI: the global alarm occurs before every applicable local policy would alarm.

The intended units are temporal epochs within replayed constructed episodes, nested in four source-IP groupings selected using full-release benign support before temporal splitting. The cohort is retrospective, not prospective, and these IP groupings are not verified physical devices/sites. Real confirmatory seeds rerandomize algorithm/training steps over the same prepared trace and episode registry; they are conditional seed-level variability, not independent network captures, organizations, or datasets. This cannot support population-level generalization uncertainty.

## Research question hierarchy

- **Primary:** On this four source-IP-grouping TON_IoT Network cohort and the predeclared constructed-episode replay, does order-1–3 complement-restricted interaction fusion improve strict global-before-local warning relative to the exact-exclusion order-1–2 predecessor, under empirical finite-horizon false-stop calibration/evaluation conditional on the fixed trace, cohort, split, and zero-event representation?
- **Secondary:** Does exact complement exclusion remove self-explanation, and does the finite order-three estimator recover the intended pure higher-order mechanism under controlled synthetic alternatives?
- **Secondary / method check:** Does the implementation reproduce exclusion-matched conditional HOFD behavior on the same target subspace?
- **Exploratory:** Which client/cohort support, benign common-mode, dropout, and strong-local-policy conditions change operational performance?
- **Diagnostics:** Scalability harness timing, synthetic validation, and comparator composition selection. These do not independently support a field generalization claim.

## Claim boundary and provisional decisions

1. The defensible methodological contribution is complement-restricted nuisance information for coalition interaction evidence, evaluated through strict ODI. HOFD, functional ANOVA, e-processes/e-detectors, and federated anomaly detection are established foundations; the acronym or their recombination is not by itself novelty.
2. The primary real-data claim is necessarily narrow: one four-source-IP-grouping TON_IoT Network cohort and a fixed constructed-episode algorithm. The roadmap's second dataset has two eligible hosts, below its stated six-source rule, so secondary controlled-trace generalization is Not Tested; its month/day-deficient timestamp cannot establish chronology, and it supplies no external replication. No physical device, multi-organization, privacy, arbitrary-drift, or deployment claim follows.
3. Synthetic seed counts and many campaign windows do not create new independent real-world sites or traces. Report inference at the level the design actually randomizes; separate conditional replay uncertainty from population generalization.
4. Protocol amendments 3–6 are explicitly outcome-informed. Their transparent documentation is good practice, but affected confirmatory runs are post-amendment analyses, not a wholly prospective preregistration. The paper must say so and show the chronology.
5. Retain the exact-exclusion vs order-1–2 paired comparison, fixed local-policy baseline, PFA calibration/held-out check, and mechanism ablations. Avoid making AUROC or comparator winner selection the primary endpoint.
6. Do not accept the old TON preprocessing identity: the six-field dedupe removed distinct raw flow records at large scale. The audit changed the duplicate identity to complete raw-row equality; every downstream artifact from the old data view must be rebuilt before evaluating claims.

## Temporal dependence diagnostic — 2026-09-25

Three development seed-coordinate runs of the production scoring-to-calibration/heldout path found strong autocorrelation in per-epoch evidence factors on the same fixed trace (lag-1 ≈ 0.957; descriptive initial-positive-lag ESS ≈ 443 from 10,920 calibration factors). Each had one false stop among 182 heldout horizons. Non-overlapping chronological horizons are disjoint windows, but that does not establish statistical independence. The diagnostic rejects blanket independence claims for per-epoch factors; it cannot estimate dependence between false-stop horizon indicators because each sparse series has only one event and the seed coordinates do not provide independent traces. The audit therefore makes no temporal thinning, horizon spacing, threshold, or acceptance-criterion change. Describe real-data false-stop results as empirical finite-horizon calibration/evaluation conditional on the fixed trace, cohort, split, and zero-event representation. This is not a population-level false-alarm guarantee and does not make the real-data route an anytime-valid e-process.

## Post-primary protocol finding — pure-order gate and TON order-three support (2026-09-26)

This prospective interpretation addendum does not overwrite the registered pure-order result, corrected TON primary, or their lifecycle/statistical artifacts.

For the pure continuous triple, let `h(u1,u2,u3) = phi1(u1) phi1(u2) phi1(u3)` and density `p_theta = 1 + theta*h` relative to independent Uniform(0,1) ranks. The implemented shifted-Legendre function has E0[phi1]=0, E0[phi1^2]=1, and E0[phi1^3]=0. Thus E0[h]=0, E0[h^2]=1, E0[h^3]=0; the target-coordinate mean under the alternative is theta, its null variance is 1, and its alternative variance is 1-theta^2. Integrating any omitted coordinate makes every proper-subset marginal exactly uniform. Since max|h|=(sqrt(3))^3, the density is nonnegative for |theta| <= 1/(3 sqrt(3)) = 0.19245009; the full configured theta grid is valid.

The registered `target_order_standardized_drift` is not the target-coordinate drift. It standardizes the change in the one-epoch `coalition_evidence_at_epoch` factor, which is a function only of the fitted 27D tensor norm. In the population oracle fit, proper-subset coefficients are zero and coordinate scales are one. The squared tensor norm factorizes as `prod_i sum_k phi_k(ui)^2`. Each factor sum is invariant under `ui -> 1-ui`; the planted `h` changes sign under that reflection. Hence `E_theta[g(norm)] - E_0[g(norm)] = theta*E_0[h*g(norm)] = 0` for every measurable norm-only score g. The null and alternative norm-score distributions coincide. Therefore the configured +0.5 target gate is impossible for the registered score even with a population-perfect fit, and is also five times the largest oracle coordinate drift. This is a protocol-specification defect independently implied by the generator and score definition, not evidence that the estimator cannot recover the interaction.

The one-seed development waterfall measured fitted-coordinate separation 0.12316 SD against oracle theta=0.1: preliminary recovery ratio 1.232, absolute error 0.02316, relative error 23.2%, and planted-coordinate correlation 0.9918. These finite-sample diagnostics are not a replicated recovery estimate. The same seed's registered factor drift was 0.00524; the second seed's earlier registered factor drift was 0.00131. Those factor drifts are not coordinate-recovery ratios. Preserve the old registered failure result; classify it as a norm-score/protocol mismatch pending a prospectively specified new identity.

The current TON cohort has four selected source-IP groupings. In every Full fitted artifact for confirmatory seeds 9000–9009, there are four order-three coalitions; the order-three context fit is `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` with zero centroids, and all four order-three coalition fits are `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` with no projection cells. Each complement has one client, below the configured minimum of two, so all four abstain before scoring. Although the operational evaluator counts 14 candidate coalitions as eligible, campaign trajectories score only four order-one coalitions. Empty order-two and order-three factor sets each aggregate to 1.0; the across-order average still includes every enabled order. All 30 confirmatory campaign rows have order-one decisive, order-two factor 1.0, and order-three factor 1.0; the third-order factor never exceeds 1 over the 1,800 campaign epochs.

Thus the measured Full global factor is `(E1 + 1 + 1)/3`, while Order-at-Most-Two is `(E1 + 1)/2`. The corrected TON trial tested the configured end-to-end Full implementation and strict-ODI endpoint on the fixed trace. It did not test a real-data order-three evidence contribution. The corrected 200/200 result, equal ODI rates, zero paired advantage, and p=1 remain unchanged. Do not interpret this structurally unavailable term as evidence against order-three usefulness; state the support limitation in any reviewer-facing account.

The corrected campaign diagnostic also shows that **neither** Full nor Order-at-Most-Two had a finite local operating policy on any of the 30 confirmatory campaign-seed rows: every row is `local_censored`, every local stop is null, and every local policy is null. The implementation of `strict_odi_outcome` explicitly returns indicator 1 when the global method stops and `earliest_local_stop_epoch` is null. Thus the recorded 30/30 Strict ODI successes per method are global detections against no finite local alarm, not observed earlier-than-local alarms. All global stops occurred at offset 1; operational lead is undefined for all rows. Preserve these registered values and the primary paired null result, while describing the current local-baseline evidence as unavailable and the Strict ODI endpoint as degenerate on this trace. A new paper's strict-before-local claim must require finite, predeclared local policies for the relevant client/campaign units; missing policies must yield Not Tested, not a success.

## Prospective sparse detector screen — 2026-09-26

The proposed high-detection claim at a 60-epoch horizon was checked against a more favorable simple-vs-simple oracle. For the true affected triple, positive direction, known effect `theta=0.1`, and null size 0.05, the exact likelihood-ratio Neyman–Pearson test had importance-weighted power 0.19119 (MCSE 0.00200 over 200,000 null paths). This is an upper bound for any method that has to estimate or search over coalition, coordinate, direction, or effect. A high detection probability at this effect and horizon is therefore unattainable; the numerical gate must not be raised by changing the effect, horizon, or false-alarm target after seeing results.

The first development-candidate score screen ran five seeds of 60-epoch oracle-atom horizons at `theta=0.1`. Each seed used 500 null calibration horizons, 500 independent null holdouts, and 500 alternatives. The sparse multiscale candidate knew the affected coalition and order, but still searched all 27 signed order-three coordinates. Its benign-calibrated threshold was 1.0 on every seed; all 500 held-out null horizons and all 500 alternative horizons per seed stayed below threshold. Thus held-out PFA was 0/500 (one-sided 95% upper bound 0.00597) and power was 0/500 (same upper bound). The pooled result is not confirmatory. Since this failure occurs with oracle atoms and more target knowledge than the full method, fitted projections and a full coalition search cannot be expected to repair it at this operating point. The candidate is retained only in `docs/audit/pocs/` and is not exposed as an active production method.

The exact-symmetry Full norm statistic has identical null and pure-triple alternative path distributions; any threshold calibrated to PFA `alpha` has power `alpha`. An order-at-most-two statistic also sees exactly null-distributed inputs under this generator. The sparse likelihood-mixture screen does not beat the calibrated baseline. A second five-seed screen of the empirically calibrated max-over-coordinate/scale scan returned mean power 0.032 (between-seed SD 0.010; mean Monte Carlo SE 0.0078) and mean held-out PFA 0.026. The descriptive power excess is 0.006, smaller than its Monte Carlo error; one seed's one-sided held-out PFA upper bound was 0.0529. It provides no reliable power lift over the lower-order and norm baselines. Keep both sparse scores as development candidates only; do not report either as a validated detector. The scan candidate must be compared against the same 60-epoch oracle bound and then the matched fitted-atom pipeline before any real-data method score.

## Population recovery gate and controlled operational benchmark — 2026-09-28

For the pure generator `p_theta = 1 + theta*phi2(u1)*phi2(u2)*phi2(u3)`, the signed target-coordinate population drift is exactly `theta`; every proper-subset population drift is zero. Report fitted recovery as signed target-coordinate drift divided by `theta`, with a root-cluster-bootstrap two-sided 95% lower endpoint greater than 0.5. Separately require the two-sided 95% upper endpoint for maximum absolute proper-subset standardized factor drift to be at most 0.1. The historical radial-factor gate and its result are preserved as historical evidence; reflection symmetry makes the radial factor population drift zero and it cannot serve as an estimator-recovery metric.

The post-primary development benchmark uses five clients, three target-generation clients, and two independent uniform complement clients. Every one of the 25 eligible coalitions is fit and scored, yielding 375 basis coordinates. The pure O3 point is `theta=.08`, `H=2,000`, `alpha=.05`; its conservative analytic density lower bound is .10557. The known-coordinate, known-sign NP power is .9734. The detector searches unknown coordinate and sign, so the matching 375-component Gaussian-mixture oracle is .43784 (MCSE .00222), not .9734. The 60%-of-excess-power floor is .282704. The separate O3 advantage requirement remains candidate power minus Order-at-Most-Two power of at least .30. The full theta-by-horizon oracle surface is retained in `docs/audit/pocs/unknown-coordinate-oracle-power-development.json`.

A prior full-coalition H=4,000 development matrix completed 30 roots: target-coordinate recovery averaged .9312 with root-bootstrap 95% CI [.8695,.9886], and proper-subset integrity passed. Its repeated-prefix alarm candidates had .0167–.0333 O3 power, held-out CP upper bounds .101–.146, and 0% O3 attribution. The issue was operational-score calibration under repeated prefix searches; the fitted population interaction was recovered. A separate H=24,000 follow-up completed six roots before being stopped; it was not pooled or used to select a method. These results remain development evidence and do not replace the current matrix.

The current comparison uses the same fitted atoms for the old radial Full, Order One, Order-at-Most-Two, and four sparse candidates. Candidate tests use the prespecified endpoint at H=2,000 and an empirical finite-horizon threshold; this supports a horizon-level alarm claim, not an early-stopping claim. The current 97.5th-percentile calibration was selected before candidate outcomes were inspected. With 300 held-out null episodes, at most 8 false stops satisfy the one-sided 95% CP upper-bound gate; if the true false-stop probability is .05, the gate passes only about 3.4% of the time. At a .025 operating point, the reproducible matching Gaussian-mixture oracle has .36278 power (MCSE .00215); the unchanged selection floor remains .282704 from the alpha=.05 oracle. This calibration clarification uses no candidate outcomes and leaves every selection gate unchanged.

The controlled benchmark is the primary mechanism-evidence dataset. The available Edge-IIoTset path remains ineligible for secondary scoring because only two client groups meet the existing eligibility contract; no stable identity/licensing-qualified real secondary dataset is claimed. The historical TON primary, its result, and its gates remain untouched and separate.

## Repaired operational comparison — 2026-09-28

All 30 repaired development roots completed and passed campaign-identity/provenance checks. The shared fitted estimator passed population recovery (mean recovery ratio 0.9519; root-bootstrap 95% CI [0.8735, 1.0289]) and proper-subset integrity (maximum-drift 95% upper endpoint 0.0296 <= 0.10). None of the four sparse operational candidates passed selection: held-out null upper bounds ranged from 0.0600 to 0.0915, all above 0.05, and pure-O3 power ranged from 0.183 to 0.217, all below the 0.282704 oracle-efficiency floor. No method is selected and no confirmatory run is authorized by the frozen selection rule.

The Old Full, Order One, and Order-at-Most-Two methods had no finite calibration terminal scores, so the aggregate's zero detections for these methods cannot support the pure-order hierarchy or an O3 advantage claim. Treat their comparisons as unavailable, not as evidence that lower orders failed. Preserve the historical TON result and all existing claim gates; do not infer operational success from estimator recovery.
