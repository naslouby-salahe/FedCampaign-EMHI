# Scientific Decisions

## Reconstructed study

FedCampaign-EMHI asks whether an interaction-based global detector can identify constructed co-temporal distributed malicious episodes before any fixed client-local detector policy acts. For each coalition, it constructs nuisance context from predictable history of clients outside that coalition, estimates the coalition's higher-order innovation after removing proper-subset structure, and aggregates bounded evidence into a global stopping rule. The operational outcome is strict ODI: the global alarm occurs before every applicable local policy would alarm.

The intended units are temporal epochs within replayed constructed episodes, nested in four source-IP groupings selected using full-release benign support before temporal splitting. The cohort is retrospective, not prospective, and these IP groupings are not verified physical devices/sites. Real confirmatory seeds rerandomize algorithm/training steps over the same prepared trace and episode registry; they are conditional seed-level variability, not independent network captures, organizations, or datasets. This cannot support population-level generalization uncertainty.

## Research question hierarchy

- **Primary:** On this four source-IP-grouping TON_IoT Network cohort and the predeclared constructed-episode replay, does order-1–3 complement-restricted interaction fusion improve strict global-before-local warning relative to the exact-exclusion order-1–2 predecessor, under empirical finite-horizon false-stop calibration/evaluation conditional on the fixed trace, cohort, split, and zero-event representation?
- **Secondary:** Does exact complement exclusion remove self-explanation, and does the finite order-three estimator recover the intended pure higher-order mechanism under controlled synthetic alternatives?
- **Secondary / method check:** Does the implementation reproduce exclusion-matched conditional HOFD behavior on the same target subspace?
- **Exploratory:** Which client/cohort support, benign common-mode, dropout, and strong-local-policy conditions change operational performance?
- **Diagnostics:** Scalability harness timing, synthetic validation, and comparator composition selection. These do not independently support a field generalization claim.

## Claim boundary and provisional decisions

1. The defensible methodological contribution is complement-restricted nuisance information for coalition interaction evidence, evaluated through strict ODI. HOFD, functional ANOVA, e-processes/e-detectors, and federated anomaly detection are established foundations; the acronym or their recombination is not by itself novelty.
2. The primary real-data claim is necessarily narrow: one four-source-IP-grouping TON_IoT Network cohort and a fixed constructed-episode algorithm. The roadmap's second dataset has two eligible hosts, below its stated six-source rule, so secondary controlled-trace generalization is Not Tested; its month/day-deficient timestamp cannot establish chronology, and it supplies no external replication. No physical device, multi-organization, privacy, arbitrary-drift, or deployment claim follows.
3. Synthetic seed counts and many campaign windows do not create new independent real-world sites or traces. Report inference at the level the design actually randomizes; separate conditional replay uncertainty from population generalization.
4. Protocol amendments 3–6 are explicitly outcome-informed. Their transparent documentation is good practice, but affected confirmatory runs are post-amendment analyses, not a wholly prospective preregistration. The paper must say so and show the chronology.
5. Retain the exact-exclusion vs order-1–2 paired comparison, fixed local-policy baseline, PFA calibration/held-out check, and mechanism ablations. Avoid making AUROC or comparator winner selection the primary endpoint.
6. Do not accept the old TON preprocessing identity: the six-field dedupe removed distinct raw flow records at large scale. The audit changed the duplicate identity to complete raw-row equality; every downstream artifact from the old data view must be rebuilt before evaluating claims.

## Verified raw-data constraints

- The configured raw root resolves to a shared external directory. `fedcampaign doctor` found 23 TON Network files and one Edge CSV. Direct raw samples confirm 46 TON fields (including `ts`, `src_ip`, `proto`, `service`, `label`, `type`) and 63 Edge fields, with year-only timestamps such as `2021 11:44:10...`.
- Direct raw census confirms the TON release has 23 files and 22.34 million rows. Four highest-support benign source-IP groupings clear the declared support rule; persisted preprocessing selects those four and records 156,664 client-epochs, 270 malicious epochs, and only three candidate campaign intervals.
- Persisted Edge preprocessing for the byte-identified CSV reports 122,782 of 2,219,201 rows with unparseable timestamps, only two eligible hosts, no selected cohort, and zero usable epochs. Its year-only timestamps do not establish calendar chronology. Secondary real-data generalization is unsupported under the current rule.
- Primary client eligibility/ranking uses benign support from the full trace before temporal splits. The detector's held-out scores are not used to fit its model, but the selected population depends on later benign data. The narrow estimand is therefore conditional on the four highest-support source-IP groupings in this release; a prospective cohort-selection claim would require selecting from pre-evaluation support only.
- The audit feasibility POC shows a pre-evaluation support-selection sensitivity can satisfy the unchanged four-client and per-client support rules, with 180 dense calibration and held-out horizons each. Its fixed support window is `[25903319, 25911841)`, borrowed from the existing primary detector+nuisance fit interval, so this is an audit-added temporal-selection sensitivity rather than prospective device/site sampling. Only 727 epochs have explicit shared benign rows, all in detector fit; later zero-filled bins do not establish capture coverage. Keep the primary cohort untouched and label any future sensitivity estimate conditional on the dense zero-fill representation.
- TON held-out benign partitions precede the first malicious interval. The study does not test post-incident benign drift or future benign operation after a campaign.
- The raw TON class mix is heavily attack-skewed overall (~96.4% malicious-labelled rows), while eligibility and model fitting use benign-only epochs. Report that population construction explicitly; do not present the selected benign cohort as a naturally balanced trace.
- The six-field TON duplicate-collision POC found over 21 million removed valid rows, mostly distinct full raw records. This was corrected in production to use complete-row identity. Explicit semantic fingerprints now mark existing preprocessing and experiment records stale; they remain historical and cannot support the corrected analysis. Comparator horizon, cohort-selection window, campaign construction/category, censoring, and statistical aggregation semantics are separately represented in typed records/dependency boundaries.
- At the 2026-09-25 checkpoint, status reported the old primary 200/200 cells as `BLOCKED / STALE`; no corrected primary campaign had yet been run. Those old result files remain historical and cannot support inference.

Raw census and code-path checks were complete at the 2026-09-25 checkpoint. The corrected rules are recorded as Roadmap amendments 7–8. The 2026-09-26 continuation below records the rebuilt current primary artifacts; no production protocol threshold or seed grid was changed on these findings.

## Audit follow-up — Edge complete-row identity (2026-09-25)

Review of the secondary loader found that its duplicate key used only the binary label and attack type after parsing, even though the configured complete CSV row was available. This could collapse distinct Edge flow rows that share those two labels, contrary to the existing complete-record rule in Roadmap §7.1. The loader now hashes the ordered complete CSV field/value pairs, and preprocessing deduplicates on that digest. A focused fixture verifies that repeated full rows collapse while rows differing in an otherwise unused field remain distinct. This is an implementation correction to the locked duplicate rule, not a new cohort or protocol choice. **Current state (2026-09-25):** corrected Edge preprocessing and its idempotent second pass completed; Edge remains `Not Tested` because the release does not meet the locked six-source eligibility rule.

## Pre-evaluation cohort-selection feasibility (2026-09-25)

The minimum 600-epoch global prefix had only one group meeting the unchanged four-group target and 5,000-record / 600-epoch requirements. A separate 8,522-epoch support window was then anchored to the primary detector-fit plus nuisance-fit interval, ending before threshold calibration and held-out evaluation. Within this fixed pre-evaluation window, exactly four raw source-IP groupings meet the same support minima and deterministic ranking rule. The production split constructor gives them 30,086 common chronological epochs, including 180 complete 60-epoch calibration horizons and 180 held-out horizons (the locked minimum is 59 each). The POC records the selected identities, window, counts, and split support under ignored `docs/audit/pocs/` artifacts; it does not fit or score methods.

This establishes feasibility for a training-only cohort-selection sensitivity, not a prospectively preregistered study. The window bounds are derived from the already locked primary split, so the analysis chronology must be disclosed. The fourth candidate is the raw source value `127.0.0.1`; it is not a fabricated grouping, but reinforces that source-IP groups are not verified devices or sites.

The support POC also found that only 727 epochs contain at least one observed benign flow row for all four sensitivity groups, all within detector fit; the later nuisance, calibration, and held-out ranges have zero *jointly observed benign-flow* epochs. The current production preprocessor fills every epoch between each selected client's first and last observed event and marks a no-event bin benign. This is part of the existing registered data contract, so the split-count gates pass, but the evidence is conditional on treating no observed flow as benign zero activity. The raw data do not establish continuous telemetry coverage. Accordingly, PFA/ODI interpretation must remain conditional on this zero-fill representation, and a reviewer-facing caveat is required. No threshold, cohort minimum, or primary cohort has been changed.

## Temporal dependence diagnostic — 2026-09-25

Three development seed-coordinate runs of the production scoring-to-calibration/heldout path found strong autocorrelation in per-epoch evidence factors on the same fixed trace (lag-1 ≈ 0.957; descriptive initial-positive-lag ESS ≈ 443 from 10,920 calibration factors). Each had one false stop among 182 heldout horizons. Non-overlapping chronological horizons are disjoint windows, but that does not establish statistical independence. The diagnostic rejects blanket independence claims for per-epoch factors; it cannot estimate dependence between false-stop horizon indicators because each sparse series has only one event and the seed coordinates do not provide independent traces. The audit therefore makes no temporal thinning, horizon spacing, threshold, or acceptance-criterion change. Describe real-data false-stop results as empirical finite-horizon calibration/evaluation conditional on the fixed trace, cohort, split, and zero-event representation. This is not a population-level false-alarm guarantee and does not make the real-data route an anytime-valid e-process.
