Closest prior-work review is preliminary, not a systematic review. Dependent-input functional ANOVA / HOFD is established ([Chastaing et al., 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.1788); [numerical estimation, 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4372)). E-detectors are also established ([Shin, Ramdas & Rinaldo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03532)), and federated anomaly detection has direct prior implementations such as [Fed-ANIDS](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2023.121000) and [Fed-ExDNN](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10064694). These works do not establish an exact match to complement-only predictable nuisance construction plus strict ODI, but the search is not exhaustive; avoid “first” claims. The novelty is a narrow combination / admissibility rule whose empirical value remains to be established.

## New protocol limitations — 2026-09-26

- **Pure-order gate is infeasible for the registered score.** The generator's oracle target-coordinate drift is exactly theta (0.1 SD at the primary theta, and at most 0.18 SD on the configured grid). The registered target metric is a one-epoch norm-derived factor. Reflection symmetry makes its population distribution identical under the pure-triple alternative and null, so its population standardized drift is zero. The old 0.5 gate remains the historical registered rule and result; its failure cannot be read as an estimator-recovery bound. See the derivation in `Scientific Decisions.md` and the code definitions in `synthetic/pure_order.py`, `evaluation/sequential.py`, and `emhi/evidence.py`.
- **The TON primary has no order-three scores.** Each of the ten confirmatory Full fit artifacts contains four order-three coalitions with `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`; each complement has one source-IP grouping while the support rule requires two. The four candidates therefore abstain, the empty order-three average is 1.0, and the global across-order average includes that neutral order. All order-two fits are also insufficient in these artifacts; each campaign trajectory scores only four order-one coalitions. The current primary tests the configured end-to-end Full pipeline under those support rules, but not real-data order-three evidence. Its result is unchanged.
- **TON Strict ODI is degenerate without local policies.** In the current ceiling reconstruction, all 60 Full/comparator campaign rows have null local policies and stops; all global stops are at offset 1; operational lead is null; and `strict_odi_outcome` defines global stop with no finite local stop as indicator 1. Preserve the registered 30/30 rates per method, but do not describe them as a demonstrated before-local warning. Future primary claims require finite local operating points, with missing local policy classified as Not Tested.

## Repaired operational comparison — 2026-09-28

- **No sparse detector passed the frozen development gates.** Across 30 roots, the four candidates had held-out false-stop one-sided 95% upper bounds of 0.0600–0.0915 and pure-O3 power of 0.183–0.217, below the required 0.05 and 0.282704 respectively. No fresh confirmation was run.
- **The lower-order baseline comparison is not interpretable.** Old Full, Order One, and Order-at-Most-Two had null calibrated thresholds because their null-calibration episodes produced no finite endpoint scores. Their aggregate zero detections must not be presented as evidence that the lower-order detectors failed or as a valid O3 added-value contrast.
- **Estimator recovery is supported separately.** Target-coordinate recovery passed (mean ratio 0.9519; root-bootstrap 95% CI [0.8735, 1.0289]) and proper-subset integrity passed (upper endpoint 0.0296). This does not establish operational detection.
