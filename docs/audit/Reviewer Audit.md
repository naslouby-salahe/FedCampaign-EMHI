Closest prior-work review is preliminary, not a systematic review. Dependent-input functional ANOVA / HOFD is established ([Chastaing et al., 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.1788); [numerical estimation, 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4372)). E-detectors are also established ([Shin, Ramdas & Rinaldo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03532)), and federated anomaly detection has direct prior implementations such as [Fed-ANIDS](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2023.121000) and [Fed-ExDNN](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10064694). Multi-stream quickest-change detection is an adjacent operational area ([Halme et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05486)). These works do not establish an exact match to complement-only predictable nuisance construction plus strict ODI, but the search is not exhaustive; avoid “first” claims. The novelty is a narrow combination / admissibility rule whose empirical value remains to be established.
